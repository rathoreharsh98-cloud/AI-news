   - In 2026, humanity has deployed thousands of radar satellites (SAR), ADS-B flight transponders, maritime AIS transceivers, seismic sensor networks, and optical earth observation satellites.

## Slide 5: The Proprietary Moat & Levchin Anti-Spoofing Architecture
### Mathematical Rigor That Cannot Be Gamed

Competitors rely on superficial LLM sentiment prompts that are vulnerable to prompt injection, viral social swarms, and GPS spoofing. VERITAS implements three mathematical invariants:

1. **Levchin Multi-Sensor Anti-Spoofing Invariant:**
   - In contested electronic warfare theaters (e.g., Black Sea, Persian Gulf), hostile actors routinely broadcast false AIS/ADS-B coordinates.
   - *Invariant:* An un-triangulated RF beacon that is unsupported by secondary physical sensors (optical, SAR, or multi-receiver TDoA arrays) is mathematically capped at $\le 75\%$ (`DEVELOPING`) regardless of viral news echoes.
2. **Deterministic Physical Contradiction Penalty:**
   - If hard physical telemetry contradicts an event (e.g., a satellite pass confirms a target is undamaged), a deterministic **$-65$ point penalty** overrides social and wire signals, forcing the dossier into `DISPUTED`.
3. **Sybil Resistance & Epistemic Diversity Weighting:**
   - 10,000 tweets or Reddit posts echoing the exact same syntactic structure from accounts $<30$ days old are throttled to contribute $\le 3\%$ to the overall corroboration score.
4. **Clean-Room AGPL-3.0 Compliance:**
   - External OSINT telemetry engines licensed under AGPL-3.0 are hosted in air-gapped sidecar containers over stateless HTTP/2 REST microservices with pure data-only JSON schemas, completely protecting VERITAS's proprietary IP.
